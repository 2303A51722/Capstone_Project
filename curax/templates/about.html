ABOUT CURAX

A connected approach to healthcare information.

CuraX is an academic healthcare-management prototype designed to organize records and support coordination among patients and healthcare stakeholders.

How it works

01

Create an account

Register as a patient, doctor, diagnostic center or insurance provider.

02

Use your workspace

Your account role determines which dashboard and actions are available.

03

Coordinate securely

Share access and documents only through authorized application workflows.

Prototype scope

This student project is a demonstration system. It is not certified for clinical use, real insurance adjudication, or production storage of sensitive medical records.
